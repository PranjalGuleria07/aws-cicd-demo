FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions our travelers ask most often.

How do I book a trip?

Fill out our contact form with your desired destination and dates. Our team will follow up within 2 hours with a personalized itinerary and quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, bank transfer, and monthly installment plans for packages over $1,000.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. 50% refund between 15–30 days. We recommend travel insurance for full peace of mind.

Do you include travel insurance?

Travel insurance can be added to any package. We partner with leading insurers to offer comprehensive coverage including medical emergencies and trip cancellation.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 3–6 months in advance for peak season travel. However, we can often accommodate last-minute trips within 4–6 weeks.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! Groups of 8 or more receive a minimum 10% discount. Corporate retreats and wedding parties get tailored pricing. Contact us for a group quote.
